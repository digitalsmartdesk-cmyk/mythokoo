What is MythoKoo?

A picture book series that tells the stories of the heroes who stood in the shadows of the epics — the ones history glossed over, but whose courage shaped everything.

Who are these books for?

Children aged 4–12, and the parents who read to them. Rich in story, brief enough for bedtime, and illustrated in full colour on every page.

Where to read

All 16 books are available on Kindle worldwide. Each title is priced at $2.99 (₹149 in India) and is part of KDP Select for Kindle Unlimited subscribers.
